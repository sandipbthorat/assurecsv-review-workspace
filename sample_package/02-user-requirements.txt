DOCUMENT: User Requirements Specification
Revision: 2.0
Approval Status: Approved
System Name: NovaQMS
Application Version: 2.4

URS-001 The system shall require each user to authenticate through corporate single sign-on before accessing quality records.

URS-002 The system shall restrict CAPA approval to users assigned the Quality Approver role and prevent record originators from acting as final approvers.

URS-003 The system shall maintain an audit trail for creation, modification, deletion, approval, and status changes. The audit trail shall record user, date and time, action, previous value, and new value.

URS-004 The system shall apply a 10-minute inactivity timeout to interactive user sessions.

URS-005 The system shall be easy to use and provide appropriate notifications as needed.

URS-006 The system shall transfer approved disposition status to MES, retry failed transfers, notify the interface owner, and prevent duplicate disposition records.

URS-007 The system shall retain approved quality records and their metadata for 15 years and provide accurate and complete PDF copies throughout the retention period.

URS-008 The system shall require electronic-signature authentication at final approval and record signer identity, signature meaning, signing date and time, and an unbreakable link to the signed record.
